## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Spoiler Alert** is a comedic action platformer developed by tinyBuild, available exclusively for **Windows**. This unique game flips platforming on its head, allowing players to experience the game in reverse, starting from the end where the boss is defeated and the coins are collected. The **latest version** includes all features that enhance your gameplay experience.

Dive into 100 levels across 4 worlds, encounter epic boss fights, and face a variety of unique enemies, all while enjoying beautiful hand-drawn graphics and an original soundtrack by Roland La Goy. **Spoiler Alert** is the full version of a game you might think you know, but with a gameplay twist that you have never tried before!
